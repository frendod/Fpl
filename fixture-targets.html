HOW IT'S RATED

Each fixture gets a difficulty that's half FPL's official FDR and half the model's team ratings (the same attack/defence ratings the xP engine uses, fitted on xG). Attackers rates a fixture by how many goals the team is expected to score; Defenders by how many it's expected to concede. The model half is rescaled to FPL's 1–5 spread (same average and spread) so the two halves weigh the same.

Avg ease = sum of (6 − difficulty) for every fixture in the window ÷ number of gameweeks. A blank adds 0, so it drags a team down; a double adds both, so it lifts them. Higher is better.

Top 3 = the three highest summed xP over the same window: MID/FWD in Attackers mode, GKP/DEF in Defenders mode. Price and expected minutes alongside; minutes in red when under 60 or the player is flagged.
