Implied probabilities are devigged from the 1X2 market. The headline score is the most likely scoreline within the outcome the market favours, so it always agrees with the bar above it. xG and clean-sheet figures are derived from the whole correct-score distribution and are the numbers worth acting on — a single scoreline rarely clears 11%, so treat it as a flavour, not a forecast. All from one book (Unibet). Each card uses its latest capture: 70 minutes before kickoff once lineups are out, otherwise the deadline price. Team news is inferred from how goalscorer odds moved between those two captures, not from an official lineup feed. Not betting advice.
